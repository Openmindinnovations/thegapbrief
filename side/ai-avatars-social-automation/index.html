Realistic AI Avatars and Instagram/TikTok Automation: The Toolchain, Real Prices and the Official Rate Limits

The realistic-avatar stack is Higgsfield or Midjourney for the face, ElevenLabs for voice, HeyGen for lip-synced video, and Zernio for posting, about $110 a month at list prices. This report covers ranked sites and prices, disclosure and likeness rules, the official API caps (Instagram 50 posts/24h; TikTok ~15 posts/day per creator, private until audit), what happened to Instagress-style bots, and compliant alternatives. It contains no evasion methods.
