5 trends to rideTop 5 trends CharacterGuard ads can ride

The Jean Phil copycat wave. A hit character now gets cloned within days.

A mustache, a suit, a barefoot billionaire. Distinctive looks are exactly what's worth protecting by name and face.

(Higgsfield Genjutsu, Seedance reference mode). The same tools let anyone drop your character into their clip.

Instagram's AI-generated profile label and TikTok's AI labels make "verified original" a credible, timely message.

Tools3 ad ideas

7.1"Spot the original" (split-screen, 9:16, about 15 s)

Four near-identical versions of a fictional CharacterGuard demo character (never a real third party's character). Hook: "Your AI influencer went viral. So did these 4." Cut to a PromptPatrol face-watch scan flagging the lookalikes. End card: "Free name-conflict check at characterguard.com."

7.2"Drop me into any trend… wait, not that one" (motion-transfer skit)

The demo character joins a trend template, then finds a clone running the same trend with a different handle. Voiceover: "Same tools that made you famous can copy you by Friday. Lock your name, watch your face." CTA: the $299 Starter (Lock kit + PromptPatrol).

7.3"Receipts" (creator-callout format)

Mirrors the comedian side-by-side. The creator holds up a dated CharacterGuard Lock record beside a copycat clip: "I don't argue, I show the timestamp." Carousel variant: Day 1 viral, Day 3 copycat, Day 4 flagged, Day 5 takedown filed.

Ad caution: don't use Jean Phil, Charles Robinson or other real accounts' likenesses in ads, and label any AI footage. Don't promise takedowns or approvals; say "watch, flag and document."
