Real product, the missing piece, and why nobody covers it yet.

1.1AI-text watermarks fade after light editing, and there is no shared detector

MissingA way for publishers, schools, or platforms to check text from any vendor, one that still works after light editing.

EvidenceOpenAI will watermark ChatGPT and Codex text in the EU to comply with the AI Act’s transparency rules. TechCrunch reports that in OpenAI’s own test, replacing 10% of words with synonyms cut detection from about 92% to 66%, and that short passages, math, and translations are harder to detect. Anthropic watermarks Claude text separately.

Why openEach lab marks its own output, and the report doesn’t describe a public checker that works across vendors.

1.2ChatGPT: users report they can no longer switch models inside a conversation

MissingSwitching to a better-suited model partway through a conversation, without starting a new chat and losing context.

EvidenceAn Oct 4 OpenAI Community bug report from a paid user says in-chat model switching disappeared and that memory is often not applied in long chats. A reply reports similar memory problems since mid-August.

Why openThe user describes it as an unannounced change. We found no explanation from OpenAI.

1.3ChatGPT web: users rely on browser extensions to see edited-message versions and export full chats

MissingBuilt-in navigation of message versions and branches, plus a complete export of a single conversation.

EvidenceA long-running OpenAI Community bug thread about the removed version arrows was still active on Oct 2. Users describe extensions breaking as the web endpoints change, and an extension developer posting fixes.

Why openUsers say third-party extensions are the only workaround, and those break each time the site changes.

AIThe most important AI updates this week

What changed and why it matters.

3.1OpenAI will watermark ChatGPT and Codex text in the EU

What changedAnnounced Oct 5. The invisible watermark rolls out over the coming weeks to EU users on all plans to meet the AI Act’s transparency rules, which took effect Aug 2. API developers anywhere can turn it on for select models; it is off by default.

Why it mattersEU rules are now changing how a major AI product works by default in one region. Products that publish AI text in the EU will need a disclosure plan.

TechCrunch

3.2OpenAI adds display ads beside generated images

What changedVisual ads will appear next to images that ChatGPT generates, starting later this month in the U.S. with an initial test group of advertisers. OpenAI also announced more measurement partners and brand-suitability pilots with DoubleVerify and IAS.

Why it mattersThe free tier is becoming an ad business with standard ad-industry measurement tools.

TechCrunch

3.3Google DeepMind watermarks AI-designed proteins (SynthID Bio)

What changedSynthID Bio embeds a signal in proteins during design. In lab tests, the marked proteins worked as well as unmarked ones. Running a marked sequence through another design tool removes the mark, and the method cannot identify who made a protein. DeepMind is releasing the code and data to researchers.

Why it mattersProvenance marking is spreading from media into biology, and so are its limits.
