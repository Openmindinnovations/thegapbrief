An AI Course for Kids Under 14: Market, Competitors, Ideas, Needs and Costs

Kids already use AI widely (86% of 9–17s per Common Sense), but free school curricula own basic literacy and paid live AI classes cluster at grade 6+ with Python. The opening is a safety-first, parent-in-the-loop, project-based course for ages 10–13. Includes competitor pricing, the amended COPPA rule, California's new chatbot laws, needs, costs and a pilot plan.
