The small features people relied on went missing. And age checks won in court.

MyFitnessPal’s per-meal carbs, DoorDash’s allergy notes and Alexa’s aisle-order list disappeared in redesigns. Plus stablecoins on Mastercard’s network and a cross-store cart, Florida’s court win and Discord lawsuit, and Gemini 4 Argon, Meta’s open weights and Character.AI’s bankruptcy.

GapsMissing pieces in real products

  1. MyFitnessPal: carbs and protein per meal are gone from the diary
  2. DoorDash on Android: the special-instructions box has disappeared
  3. Smart TVs: no setting for “my apps, my HDMI inputs, nothing else”
  4. Alexa app: the shopping list can’t be put in store order anymore
  5. Google Maps: police and crash alerts from hours or days ago won’t expire
  6. Dunkin’ app: the rebuild made reordering and card reloads harder

MarketsEmerging in apps and digital products

  1. Stablecoin balances you can spend on a card network
  2. Banks renting a fintech’s entire platform under their own brand
  3. One shopping cart across many retailers
  4. Inheritance tools built into bank accounts
  5. Health tracking without a new wearable, and paid prevention plans
  6. Proof that resold and luxury goods are real
  7. Visa and entry checks handled at the moment of booking

KidsKids' apps: popular, growing, missing

  1. Popular: YouTube Kids now tops both US kids’ charts
  2. Missing: real age checks and safe defaults on kids’ chat platforms
  3. Demand driver: Florida can now enforce its under-14 social media law
  4. Growing: parents can share an age range with apps, not a birthdate
  5. Growing: apps where kids make the story instead of scrolling a feed
  6. Growing: kids’ coding and AI classes expand across borders

AIThe week's key AI updates
